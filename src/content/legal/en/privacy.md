## 6. Retention and deletion
### 6.1 Account deletion
1. You request deletion from "Account" while signed in and confirm with a link sent to your email (valid 30 minutes, single use).
2. On confirmation your access is **revoked immediately and irreversibly**; sessions are closed.
3. Cleanup starts and may continue **asynchronously**: it removes your profile, posts, likes, uploaded files and your credentials. **We do not claim that everything disappears instantly.**
4. If a step fails, **the account stays blocked** and cleanup remains pending; a cleanup error never restores access.
5. Failures are logged and raise alerts for the technical team. Today a failed cleanup **may require a manual retry**. **We do not promise unattended automatic retries.**
6. Your username becomes free without anyone inheriting anything from your account. Accounts with a team role cannot be deleted via self-service.

### 6.2 What is deleted and what is kept
- **Deleted** (in the cleanup above): profile, posts, likes, files used only by your posts and the sign-in identity. Files shared with another resource are kept until released.
- **Kept**, under restricted access, not shown publicly and linked to an internal identifier: reports, moderation cases and decisions, strikes (which, while the account exists, are used to apply the sanctions system), moderation audit records, copyright claims and appeals (including the appeal contact, not anonymised), the deletion record (no email or token) and the acceptance evidence (version and date). **We have not set a retention period and there is no automatic purge of these records, and we do not promise deletion periods.** We keep them as long as needed for security, defence against claims, traceability or legal obligations.
- The deletion token (only its hash) expires after 30 minutes.

### 6.3 Existing copies
Backups and manual copies of the database, our own or our providers', may exist and contain data of accounts that have already been deleted. They are not deleted immediately or automatically, and we do not promise a rotation schedule.

### 6.4 Exceptions
We may keep data when necessary to comply with a legal obligation, handle or defend a claim, investigate a security incident or prevent abuse, in a justified and limited way.

### 6.5 Data managed by UPMINA Web and data processed by third parties
The above describes the data UPMINA Web stores directly (database and files). Providers keep data under their own policies and periods.
