## 4. Copyright claims procedure
- **Copyright claim** ([/copyright](/copyright)): submitted by the **rights holder** (or representative) when a Community post infringes their work. No account required. Public form with name, email, organisation (optional), relationship to the work, description of the work, link to the post, description of the infringement, evidence links and a truthfulness declaration with signature; text and links only. Submitting it **does not remove anything automatically**: a team member reviews it.
- **Post report** (menu on the post itself, account required): for content that breaches the [Community rules](/community-rules). It does not replace a copyright claim.
- **What happens:** (1) review by a person; (2) if appropriate, the post is **hidden** (files are not deleted) and the author is emailed with the reason, the reference and a unique appeal link; (3) the author has **24 hours** from when the notice is confirmed as sent, one appeal per claim; (4) if the appeal is accepted, the post is restored (only if it is as the removal left it); if rejected, it stays hidden; (5) final deletion is manual, only once the deadline has passed, the notice was sent and no appeal is pending; (6) a copyright strike is manual, only after final deletion, and counts together with Community strikes. There is no automatic deletion on expiry.
- **Data:** claimant and author data are used only to handle the claim and kept as restricted-access evidence (see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy)). Claim information may be communicated to the affected author.
- Claims must be made in good faith. Contact: **upminaaweb@gmail.com**.
