# Community Rules — DRAFT v0.3 (unpublished · working translation · pending review)

> Working translation of `NORMAS-DE-COMMUNITY.borrador.md`. These rules describe **only** the mechanisms UPMINA Web implements today. Anything that does not exist is stated as such.

## 1. Spirit
Community is a space for fans. Treat others with respect and post only your own content or content you have the right to share.

## 2. What is not allowed
- **Harassment, threats, discrimination and hostility** towards people or groups, and hate speech.
- **Spam and manipulation**: repetitive, misleading or off-topic posts.
- **Impersonation**: pretending to be UPMINA, the team or third parties; usernames that imitate other people (the system automatically blocks some impersonations).
- **Third-party privacy**: personal data, private images or doxxing.
- **Explicit sexual, violent or illegal content.**
- **AI-generated content**: a specific report reason exists. `[PENDING: decide whether it is prohibited, must be disclosed or is only reportable]`
- **Content from others** without permission and material that infringes copyright.

## 3. Reporting a post (this is not a copyright claim)
Anyone with an account can report a post, choosing a reason (spam, harassment, hate speech, sexual content, violence, off-topic, AI-generated or other) and adding an explanation of up to 1000 characters. You cannot report your own post. The reporter is not shown to the author. On reporting, the post may be hidden provisionally until reviewed.

## 4. Moderation
A team member reviews each case and decides **"Proceeds"** (the post is removed; its files are deleted after 72 hours) or **"Does not proceed"** (it is restored). The author receives a notice of the decision. Decisions are recorded and audited.

## 5. Strike system
A strike is issued by a team member, tied to a "Proceeds" decision (or a final copyright removal). The level depends on how many **non-revoked** strikes you have:

| Strike | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 1st | You cannot create or edit posts for **14 days** |
| 2nd | The same for **1 month** (calendar, UTC) |
| 3rd and later | **Permanent** posting restriction |

- Community and copyright strikes **count together**. A strike that expires by time still counts towards the next level; it stops counting only if **revoked**.
- Revoking a strike is for Developer or Admin, and cannot be done by whoever applied it.
- A decision produces at most one strike. Copyright strikes are manual and only after final deletion.
- The restriction affects posting (creating, editing, reordering or detaching media). `[CONFIRM whether the text should list exactly what remains allowed, e.g. viewing and liking]`

## 6. Copyright, appeals and restoration
- **Copyright**: handled through `/copyright` (a public form for rights holders, distinct from the ordinary report in a post's menu; see the copyright claims procedure). If a post is removed, the author has **24 hours** to appeal with the unique link received by email; if the appeal is accepted, it is restored.
- **Community strikes**: there is currently **no** dedicated appeal procedure; revocation is decided by Developer/Admin. `[DECISION PENDING: will a contact or appeal channel be offered? Nothing will be promised until it exists.]`

## 7. Repeat offences
Strike escalation (section 5) is the only repeat-offence mechanism implemented. `[PENDING: decide whether accounts will be terminated for serious breaches]`

## 8. Minors and visibility
An account requires at least 13 years of age and, between 13 and 17, the legal obligations of your country (see Terms §3). Your profile and posts are **public**. Do not post personal data or images of minors without the authorisation of whoever has custody. This section is a documentary clarification: it changes neither the rules nor the sanctions.
